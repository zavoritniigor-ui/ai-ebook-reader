# Third-party data

`js/cefr-lexicon-data.js` (CEFR levels for the reading statistics) is generated by `tools/build_cefr_lexicon.py` from:

- **CEFR-J Vocabulary Profile 1.5**, compiled by Yukio Tono, Tokyo University of Foreign Studies (http://www.cefr-j.org/). Free for research and commercial use with citation. Retrieved via Open Language Profiles (https://github.com/openlanguageprofiles/olp-en-cefrj).
- **Octanove Vocabulary Profile C1/C2 1.0**, Octanove Labs. CC BY-SA 4.0 (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/).
- **FLELex** (TreeTagger, Beacco version): François, T., Gala, N., Watrin, P. & Fairon, C. (2014), *FLELex: a graded lexical resource for French foreign learners*, LREC 2014; Pintard, A. & François, T. (2020). CENTAL, UCLouvain (https://cental.uclouvain.be/cefrlex/flelex/). CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc-sa/4.0/) — non-commercial use only.

The generated file keeps only the word → level mapping (lowest level per word); multi-word entries are dropped.
